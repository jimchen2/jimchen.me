The mornings are cool enough now that I open the window before I make coffee. Nothing
happened today, in the best possible way, and I wanted to write that down before the
week swallows it.

## Morning

I walked to the bakery on the corner and the woman behind the counter remembered my
order, which felt like an unreasonable amount of joy for a pastry. I sat outside and
watched two dogs negotiate custody of a tennis ball for eleven minutes. Neither of
them was willing to admit that the other one had won.

Back home I finally fixed the squeaky hinge on the closet door. It took four months to
think about it, and about forty seconds to do it. This ratio seems to hold for most
household tasks.

## Afternoon

I read for a while with the window open — *The Changeling* by Joy Williams, which I keep
putting down because a sentence knocks the wind out of me, then picking up again.

> A book that makes you stop reading for a minute is doing its job better than one that
> never does.

I made a note of that, then made another note that said *stop noting things and read*.

At some point I fell asleep on the couch, which I have decided to count as a productive
activity.

## Evening

Dinner was noodles with too much ginger, a mistake I intend to repeat. I called my
parents and my mother described, in detail, the neighbor's new fence. I described my
fence research, which is currently limited to noticing that fences exist.

Some small things I don't want to forget:

- The light at 6:40 p.m. is now horizontal and gold.
- There is a cat that sits in the third-floor window of the building across the street
  every evening, like a small supervisor.
- I have started saying good morning to the guy who sweeps the sidewalk outside the
  noodle place. Neither of us knows the other's name and we have agreed this is fine.

Tomorrow the week starts again. Tonight is enough.
